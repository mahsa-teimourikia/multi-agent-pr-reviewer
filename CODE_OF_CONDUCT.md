# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in ReviewForge welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good intent, explain disagreements clearly, respect different viewpoints, and protect private information and credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted contact, publishing private information, and misuse of repository access are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially, and maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action necessary to keep the project safe.
